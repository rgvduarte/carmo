Gonçalo Carmo · 50-word bio (60) · English

Gonçalo Carmo is a Lisbon triathlete with LB Squad Portugal. He grew up racing motocross, with over ten seasons in Portugal and Spain. His first triathlon was in May 2025; 17 months later he finished IRONMAN Portugal-Cascais in 9:35:08. In 2027 he has seven races in Portugal, Spain and Japan, with a slot for Kona in 2028 as the goal.
