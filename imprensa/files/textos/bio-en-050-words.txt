Gonçalo Carmo · 50-word bio (50) · English

Gonçalo Carmo is a Lisbon triathlete who trains with LB Squad Portugal. He grew up racing motocross, with more than ten seasons in Portugal and Spain. He did his first triathlon in May 2025 and, 17 months later, finished IRONMAN Portugal-Cascais in 9:35:08. His goal is Kona, once he qualifies.
