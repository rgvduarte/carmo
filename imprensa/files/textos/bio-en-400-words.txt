Gonçalo Carmo · 400-word bio (416) · English

Gonçalo Carmo is a triathlete from Lisbon who races for LB Squad Portugal under coach Lino Barruncho.

His story starts on a motocross track. He first raced at nine and spent more than ten seasons in the Portuguese and Spanish national championships. That is where he learned to train methodically and to work with sponsors. “In motocross my sponsors were part of the team,” he says. He left motocross in 2021.

He tried triathlon in 2025 and from the start the bike and the run came naturally. He did his first triathlon on 24 May, the Triatlo Longo de Caminha, in 4:32:54, fourth in his age group. In October he finished IRONMAN 70.3 Cascais in 4:27:16, 31st of 2,353 athletes. In November he ran the Meia Maratona dos Descobrimentos in Lisbon in 1:16:56.

His second year was the climb. In February 2026 he ran 2:44:13 at the Seville Marathon. In April he was second in M25-29 at the Lidl Setúbal Triathlon, in 4:26:49, 11th of 802 overall. In June he ran the Corrida de Santo António, a 10 km race in Lisbon, in 33:48. In September he won his age group at the Sines Triathlon, 6th of 743 overall. With Setúbal and Sines combined, he won his age group in the HMS Triathlon Series, which earned him both 2027 entries.

On 17 October 2026, 17 months after his first triathlon, he completed the full distance at IRONMAN Portugal-Cascais in 9:35:08. He was 52nd of 1,981 athletes from 71 countries and 12th of 321 in M25-29. He swam 3.8 km in 1:01:42, rode 180 km in 4:56:43 at an average of 36.4 km/h, the 29th fastest bike split of the race, and ran the marathon in 3:27:22. He finished in the top 3% overall at Setúbal, Sines and Cascais. Swimming is where he says he has the most room to grow, and that is his main focus for 2027.

He trains twice a day, around 21 hours a week, across all three disciplines. Away from training, his personal brand is GOCARMO, from “Go, Carmo!”, with a symbol of a full moon catching the sun, and he trains to J. Cole, Drake, Justin Bieber, Dillaz, Plutónio, Papillon, Regula, Slow J and X-Tense.

In 2027 he has seven races in Portugal, Spain and Japan, including Setúbal, Sines and IRONMAN Japan South Hokkaido on 12 September, where he goes for a World Championship slot. His goal is to make triathlon his living and reach Kona in 2028, once he qualifies.
