Short bio50 words

Gonçalo Carmo is a Lisbon triathlete with LB Squad Portugal. He grew up racing motocross, with over ten seasons in Portugal and Spain. His first triathlon was in May 2025; 17 months later he finished IRONMAN Portugal-Cascais in 9:35:08. In 2027 he has seven races, with a Kona slot as the goal.

Medium bio150 words

Gonçalo Carmo is a triathlete from Lisbon. He started racing motocross at nine and spent more than ten seasons in the Portuguese and Spanish national championships, until 2021. That is where he first learned to train with method.

He tried triathlon in 2025. He did his first in Caminha on 24 May and in October finished IRONMAN 70.3 Cascais in 4:27:16. In 2026 he won the M25-29 age group at Sines, took second in his age group at Setúbal and finished in the top 3% overall at both. On 17 October he completed the full distance at IRONMAN Portugal-Cascais in 9:35:08, 52nd of 1,981 athletes.

Cycling and running are his strengths. He ran 2:44:13 at the Seville Marathon and posted the 29th fastest bike split in Cascais. He trains with LB Squad Portugal under coach Lino Barruncho. In 2027 he races a qualifying IRONMAN, with Kona as the next goal.
